My approach

A clear process.
Useful decisions at every step.

Each stage ends with something concrete to review. The depth of research, testing, and implementation support depends on the agreed scope.

  1. 01

    Discover the problem

    Understand the audience, business goals, existing experience, and technical constraints. Research activities are chosen to fit the project and access to users.

    What you can review

    A shared brief, priorities, assumptions, and research plan.

  2. 02

    Define the structure

    Map the information architecture and key journeys. Use flows, sitemaps, and wireframes to review the logic before visual detail.

    What you can review

    An agreed structure and the priority flows to design.

  3. 03

    Design & refine

    Develop high-fidelity interfaces, interaction prototypes, and reusable components. Review feedback and cover responsive, loading, empty, and error states.

    What you can review

    A coherent interface and documented interaction decisions.

  4. 04

    Deliver & collaborate

    Prepare organized design files, assets, and specifications. Work with developers to review implementation and address design questions.

    What you can review

    Developer-ready files, component guidance, and an agreed handoff.

Working together

One project or a longer partnership.

A defined project works well when you have a clear goal. Ongoing collaboration supports an evolving backlog. In both cases, we agree on priorities, capacity, review rounds, and handoff responsibilities.
