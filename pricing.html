Before we start

Clear expectations.
Better collaboration.

Are these fixed prices?

These are starting estimates for the scope described. The final quote depends on complexity, unique layouts, workflows, content readiness, and implementation support. Nothing is booked or charged from this page.

What counts as a page or screen?

A unique layout or primary task screen counts toward scope. Repeated content using the same template is discussed separately. Interaction states, responsive variants, and additional roles are clarified in the proposal.

Is development included?

The listed packages cover design. Frontend implementation, CMS integration, hosting, and other development needs are scoped and quoted separately.

How do revisions and timelines work?

We agree on review rounds, milestones, and timing before starting. New features or scope changes are reviewed together before any additional work is added.

Can you work with my developers?

Yes. Organized design files and handoff notes support implementation. The level of ongoing collaboration and implementation review is agreed in the project scope.

Can we start with one workflow?

Yes. A focused engagement can address a landing page or core product flow first, with later phases quoted once priorities are clearer.
