The Challenge

Urban commuters face a fragmented experience when switching between ride-sharing and food delivery apps. The challenge was to unify these services into a single, cohesive super-app without creating cognitive overload.

Our research indicated that 65% of users struggled with navigation in existing competitor apps, particularly during high-stress commute times. The goal was to reduce the "time to action" by 40% while maintaining a visually premium aesthetic.

The Solution

We developed a modular design system centered around high-contrast legibility and "thumb-zone" navigation.

01 Unified Architecture

Merged 3 separate user flows into one dashboard, using contextual cards that predict user intent based on time of day.

02 High Visibility Mode

Implemented a dynamic contrast system that adjusts UI brightness and font weight based on ambient light sensors.

The Impact

+45% User Retention
